## 1\. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

### 1.1 Project Overview

**Chakramandal Education Private Limited**, spearheaded by Woman Entrepreneur **Ms. Indrani Chakraborty**, proposes to scale and formalize an integrated physical-and-digital educational ecosystem in **Bowbazar, Krishnanagar, Nadia (West Bengal)** under two synergistic brands:

1. **Infini Education (Units 1 & 2):** A modern, concept-driven academic coaching centre for students from **Class 2 to Class 12** across **ICSE, CBSE, WBBSE, and WBCHSE** curricula. Unlike conventional rote-learning tuition centres, Infini Education integrates physical science laboratories (Physics, Chemistry, Biology models) and computerized evaluation systems into routine after-school learning.  
2. **The Legend Group (Unit 3):** A dedicated **Information Technology (IT), Computer Application, Robotics/IoT, and 3D Printing Innovation Hub** offering practical digital literacy, programming, hardware prototyping, and vocational computer courses to school/college students and job-seeking youth in Nadia district.

### 1.2 Current Operational Status & Traction

* **Premises:** The enterprise has secured three commercial units in a prime educational cluster at Bowbazar, Krishnanagar (in close proximity to prominent schools such as Krishnagar Academy):  
  * **Unit 1:** 5 Classrooms with integrated Physics and Chemistry practical lab stations \+ 1 Administrative Office \+ Washroom.  
  * **Unit 2:** 3 Classrooms \+ 1 Dedicated Computer Lab \+ 1 Administrative Office \+ Washroom.  
  * **Unit 3 (The Legend Group Wing):** Dedicated 14-workstation IT, IoT & 3D Printing Maker Lab \+ Smart Classroom \+ Live Streaming Studio.  
* **Initial Traction:** Operations have commenced at a pilot scale with **15 enrolled students** across multiple classes, validating local parental demand for practical, lab-supported tutoring.

### 1.3 Financial Distinction: Total Enterprise Outlay vs. Bankable Project Cost

In strict adherence to Reserve Bank of India (RBI) and commercial bank lending norms, a clear distinction is made between the **Total Enterprise Financial Outlay (₹34.70 Lakh)** and the **Bank-Eligible New Project Cost (₹16.50 Lakh)**:

* Standard MSME project loans (covered under CGTMSE or Stand-Up India) finance **productive new capital assets (CapEx)** and **legitimate operating working capital (OpEx)**.  
* Existing liabilities (**₹11.20 Lakh**), large refundable landlord deposits/long-term cash buffers (**₹5.00 Lakh** out of the ₹10.00 Lakh reserve), and past statutory/ROC regularization outlays (**₹2.00 Lakh**) cannot be bundled into a fresh MSME project Term Loan.  
* Accordingly, this DPR presents a clean, bank-compliant **Eligible Project Cost of ₹16.50 Lakh** seeking **₹13.20 Lakh in Bank Credit (80%)**, while transparently disclosing the existing ₹11.20 Lakh debt obligation in the consolidated Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR) and cash flow analysis.

## 4\. MARKET FEASIBILITY & DEMAND ANALYSIS (KRISHNANAGAR, NADIA)

### 4.1 Local Demographic & Educational Landscape

* **District Headquarters Advantage:** Krishnanagar is the administrative and educational headquarters of Nadia district in West Bengal. Bowbazar is a dense residential-cum-commercial hub well-connected by local transit.  
* **Proximity to Feeder Schools:** The institute is strategically positioned near premier schools including **Krishnagar Academy**, Bishop Morrow School, Krishnagar Collegiate School, Queen of the Missions School, and multiple WBBSE/WBCHSE institutions.  
* **Market Gap Identified:**  
  1. **Unorganized Home Tutors:** Over 80% of local tutoring in Krishnanagar operates out of cramped domestic spaces with 30–50 students packed into a single room, offering zero practical lab exposure.  
  2. **Lack of Emerging Tech Training:** Local computer centers still teach outdated basic diploma courses. No local institute in Bowbazar offers hands-on **3D Printing (Bambu Lab A1)** and **IoT hardware prototyping** alongside standard academic subjects.  
  3. **NEP 2020 Alignment:** The National Education Policy (NEP 2020\) and revised CBSE/ICSE board exam patterns heavily weight competency-based, application-oriented questions (up to 50%) and coding/computational skills—driving parents toward structured, lab-equipped institutes.

## 12\. GST, INCOME TAX & STATUTORY COMPLIANCE FRAMEWORK

### 12.1 Goods and Services Tax (GST) Applicability for Coaching & IT Training

1. **Why Commercial Coaching is Taxable (Not Exempt):**  
   * Under **Entry 66 of Notification No. 12/2017–Central Tax (Rate)**, GST exemption applies strictly to recognized "Educational Institutions" providing pre-school to higher secondary education or qualifications recognized by Indian law, or vocational courses approved by NSDC/NCVET.  
   * Private academic coaching centres (**Infini Education**) and commercial computer/IT training centres (**The Legend Group**) do **not** fall under Entry 66\.  
2. **Applicable SAC Code & GST Rate:**  
   * **SAC Code:** **999293** *(Commercial training and coaching services)*.  
   * **GST Rate:** **18%** (9% CGST \+ 9% SGST for intra-state supply within West Bengal). Note: Following the GST rate rationalization in September 2025, commercial coaching under SAC 999293 remains in the standard **18% slab**.  
3. **Threshold Limit vs. Strategic Voluntary Registration:**  
   * Under **Section 22 of the CGST Act, 2017**, registration is mandatory in West Bengal once aggregate annual turnover exceeds **₹20.00 Lakh** (which Infini Education is projected to cross in Year 1 at ₹23.30 Lakh).  
   * **CA Recommendation — Voluntary Registration Before CapEx Purchase:** The company must obtain GST registration **voluntarily under Section 25(3)** *prior* to purchasing the ₹9.50 Lakh computer/lab hardware. Every vendor tax invoice must bear the company's GSTIN.

### 12.2 Input Tax Credit (ITC) Optimization on ₹9.50 Lakh Computer Setup & Commercial Rent

Because the institute renders 18% taxable output services (SAC 999293), it is entitled to claim full **Input Tax Credit (ITC) under Section 16 of the CGST Act** on capital goods and input services:

### 12.3 GST on Commercial Rent (Forward Charge vs. Reverse Charge Rule)

* **If Landlord is GST-Registered:** The landlord charges 18% GST on the monthly rent invoice (Forward Charge). The institute pays the rent \+ 18% GST and claims the 18% GST as Input Tax Credit (ITC).  
* **If Landlord is Unregistered (Crucial Notification No. 09/2024–CT(R) w.e.f. 10.10.2024):** When a GST-registered coaching institute rents commercial premises from an **unregistered landlord**, the institute (tenant) is legally required to pay **18% GST under Reverse Charge Mechanism (RCM)** in cash via Form GSTR-3B, and can immediately claim **100% ITC** of that RCM payment in the same return.

### 12.4 GST Invoicing & Return Filing Discipline

* **Composite Supply Rule (Section 8 of CGST Act):** When study materials, printed notes, or lab access are bundled with coaching tuition, it constitutes a "Composite Supply" where coaching (SAC 999293\) is the principal supply. The entire fee bundle attracts **18% GST** (splitting invoices arbitrarily to claim book exemptions under HSN 4901 invites audit penalties unless books are sold separately on an optional, standalone basis).  
* **Monthly/Quarterly Returns:** File **GSTR-1** (outward supplies) by the 11th of the subsequent month and **GSTR-3B** (summary return & tax payment) by the 20th of the subsequent month (or opt for the QRMP scheme up to ₹5 Crore turnover).

### 12.5 Income Tax, TDS & ROC Compliance (Companies Act, 2013\)

* **Tax Deduction at Source (TDS):**  
  * **Section 194-I (Rent):** Deduct 10% TDS on building rent if aggregate rent paid to a single landlord exceeds ₹50,000/month (or applicable threshold under amended Finance Act provisions).  
  * **Section 194J (Professional Fees):** Deduct 10% TDS on payments to visiting/contractual faculty exceeding ₹50,000 per annum.  
* **Annual ROC Filings (Budgeted under the ₹2.00 Lakh Compliance Head by Promoter):**  
  * **Form ADT-1** (Auditor Appointment), **DIR-3 KYC** (Directors' KYC by Sept 30), **Form AOC-4** (Financial Statements within 30 days of AGM), **Form MGT-7 / MGT-7A** (Annual Return within 60 days of AGM), and statutory maintenance of Registers and Minutes.
